Egg Hunt

This webpage is used to play the game Egg Hunt. This game was made by Llama Dev using GDevelop. In this game, you collect eggs to try to become the egg hunt champion and also, try to complete achievements to unlock new hats. All hats can be equipped together to make unique hat combinations. You can choose to play against one to four other egg hunters, or you can select custom mode to choose between many different hunters to play against. You unlock more hunters in custom mode by collecting eggs.

Hats

Hats are rewards you receive from completing tasks. Every hat starts with a question mark icon over them; however, when you unlock the hat, the question mark will disappear. Then, you will be able to equip and unequip the hat by clicking on it. You can find out how to unlock a hat by clicking on the magnifying glass located on the left side. Then, move it to the question mark and click on it to find out how to unlock the hat. Also, after unlocking a hat, you can donate it by pressing on the box, followed by the hat. After a hat is donated, you will have to unlock it again. There is no limit on how many hats you can wear at once, so make your own look. Every match, your hats will be reset to not being worn. To prevent this, press the lock on the side to keep your hats the same game to game.

Crowns

Crowns are like hats; however, they are harder to achieve. You can access the crown menu by clicking the safe in the hat menu. Crowns cannot be donated. Unlike hats, you can only wear one crown at a time. Wearing a crown will change how all eggs look.

How to unlock the crowns

Golden Crown

To unlock this crown, you must win the egg hunt with 24 eggs.

Bunny Crown

To unlock this crown, you must collect 100 eggs overall.

Baby Crown

To unlock this crown, you must collect zero eggs in baby mode while having the unicorn’s power.

Nightmare Crown

To unlock this crown, you must beat nightmare mode and scary mode at the same time.

Crimson Crown

To unlock this crown, you must win in scary mode with the one other hunter option selected.

Chaos Crown

To unlock this crown, you must have every effect in the game enabled at the same time. (Note: both the lightning and the landmines provide the same effect.)

Emerald Crown

To unlock this crown, you must donate 20 hats at once.

Egg Hunt Crown

After unlocking all the other crowns, you have a chance to see this crown on the title page. Whenever it appears, click on it to unlock this crown.

Profile

The profile menu allows you to customize your player model. You can change the color of your skin tone, shirt, pants, hair, and shoes. There are also many different hair styles and options for clothes. On this page, you can also change your player’s name. Additionally, you can see how many eggs you have collected overall. The colors are chosen using the RGB color system.

Multiplayer

This game uses GDevelop servers to make multiplayer work. All hats are disabled in this mode. To use this mode, click on the globe. Then, set up a game. Next, you can choose to join someone else online or make your own game with your current settings. If you lose without the game ending in this mode, you will turn into a specific monster determined by the way in which you lost. You can become a shadow creature, a ghost, or a zombie. If all players turn into a monster, the game ends, and no one wins. Typically, once all the eggs are gone, the game shows who won and how many eggs everyone collected on a leader board.
