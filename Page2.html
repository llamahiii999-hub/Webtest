Wizard's Quest

On this webpage, you will be able to play A Wizard's Quest by Llama Dev. This game was created in Twine. In A Wizard's Quest, you live in a village surrounded by a magical force field that stops more monsters from entering the area. Marvin the Great created the force field to stop The Shadow King from using an army of monsters to destroy the town. When he made the force field, it used the last of his power, so after creating it, he disappeared, leaving only his staff behind. It is said that the staff still sits at the top of the tallest mountain, waiting for someone to use it once more. The town has lived like this for many years; however, now, to the town’s knowledge, all the food inside the force field has already been gathered and eaten.
