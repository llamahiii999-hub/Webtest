Food Chain

This webpage is the place to play the game Food Chain by Llama Dev. This game was Llama Dev's first complete game made on GDevelop. This game is no longer updating for now. In this game, you are a fish. By eating smaller fish, you get bigger. When you become the biggest fish, you win and become the top of the food chain; however, be careful, the bigger fish are very hungry and will eat anything in their path.

Modes

There are four different modes you can turn on and off at will. Each mode is a new challenge. By winning the modes, you can unlock new characters with new abilities.

Fishing Day

In this mode, fishing bobbers are in the ocean. Make sure to stay away from them, or you might get caught.

Caffeinated Fish

In this mode, all the fish are very awake and angry. All of them had a bit too much coffee today, so they move at double their normal swim speed. Be careful, and try to avoid the lightning-fast fish that are trying to eat you.

Spawn Clams

In this mode, clams are now on the ocean floor. From time to time, these clams make bubbles. Be sure to avoid getting too close to these bubbles. Bubbles will go up forever and take anything that touches them with them. So, be sure to stay away from them.

Night Time

This mode has the game take place at night. Night makes everything very dark and harder to see.
