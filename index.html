Food Chain

This game was Llama Dev's first complete game made on GDevelop. This game is no longer updating for now. In this game, you are a fish. By eating smaller fish, you get bigger. When you become the biggest fish, you win and become the top of the food chain; however, be careful, the bigger fish are very hungry and will eat anything in their path.

Egg Hunt

This game was made by Llama Dev using GDevelop. In this game, you collect eggs to try to become the egg hunt champion and also, try to complete achievements to unlock new hats. All hats can be equipped together to make unique hat combinations. You can choose to play against one to four other egg hunters, or you can select custom mode to choose between many different hunters to play against. You unlock more hunters in custom mode by collecting eggs.

Wizard's Quest

In A Wizard's Quest you live in a village surrounded by a magical force feild to stop more monsters form entering the area. The force feild was created by Marvin the Great to stop the shadow king from useing a army of monsters to destroy the town. When he made the force feild it used the last of his power so after creating it he disapeared leaving only his staff behind. It is said that the staff still sits at the top of the tallest mounten waiting for someone to use it once more. The town has lived like this for many years however now all the food in the force feild has already been gathered and eatten to the towns knowldge.

Infinite Art

This game was created for fun, and is not a serious art program. In Infinite Art, you use different tools to draw. After you are done drawing, clicking the save button will save your art. Then, using the save menu, you can reload your art. You have the ability to change the color with the RGB color system. You can change the size of your pen to draw thicker or thinner lines. Using the eye drop tool lets you select a color you have already used. The trash can allows you to erase a spot where you have drawn. Pressing the delete key on your keyboard, clears what you have drawn. Moving the arrow keys allows you to view more of the canvas.
